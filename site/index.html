A media viewer for galleries that have galleries in them.

Product colours with their angles, review moments with their screens, listings with their rooms. Gallerist treats each group as a place you move between and each item as a place inside it.

Two axes, not one long strip

Most viewers flatten everything into a line. Here a collection is a sheet: rows are groups, and each row holds its own items. This sheet is driven by the headless core with no renderer at all, the same state the viewer above runs on.

A good fit if

  • your media is really two-level, not one list
  • people review it: comments pinned to a screenshot, a timeline of captures
  • you have thousands of groups, or use the flat rail: the DOM stays bounded
  • keyboard, touch, RTL and screen readers all matter

Look elsewhere if

  • you have one flat strip and a lightbox you already like
  • you need Vue or Svelte today: it's React and plain DOM, with Vue planned for 0.4
  • you want a stories player
  • you need a stable API: this is pre-1.0, and minor versions can still change it
